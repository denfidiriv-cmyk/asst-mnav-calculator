How the number is built

mNAV is the share price divided by NAV per common share. NAV is the value of the Bitcoin minus what preferred holders are owed. The steps below use your current inputs, so every figure changes when you change a field.

    What this does not do

    • No cash flows. Preferred dividends, interest, fees, operating costs and taxes are left out. All of them would lower NAV per share.
    • Simplified financing. Preferred is valued at par. New preferred is raised in proportion to the Bitcoin bought, so claims end at the share of BTC value you chose. New common is sold at the multiple you chose, every time.
    • One price path. Bitcoin moves in a smooth curve from the start price to the ending price. A real path with swings would give different dilution.
    • Not a forecast and not financial advice. The result says what has to be true for a price, not that it will happen.
    • Credit. The idea of asking "what mNAV does a price target need" comes from the Grain of Salt SF calculator. This page uses its own simplified model, so its numbers will not match theirs exactly.
