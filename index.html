How it is calculated
    Assumptions and limits

    Company numbers come from Strive's latest 8-K and are checked twice a week. Prices are live. Replace any number with your own.

    • Left out by default. Dividends on the preferred already outstanding, new common shares, operating costs and taxes. All of them would lower net assets per share.
    • Simplified new SATS. It is sold today, a fixed share of its face value buys Bitcoin at today's price, and one year of its dividends is deducted.
    • Preferred at par. Claims are counted at face value.
    • mNAV on this page. The ASST price divided by net assets per share today: Bitcoin held at the live price plus cash, minus preferred claims, divided by common shares. Other sources define mNAV differently, so their numbers can differ.
    • Scenario, not forecast. The page shows what has to be true for a result. It is not financial advice.
