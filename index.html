How it is calculated
    Assumptions you can change

    Defaults are the starting numbers behind the net-asset grid in the author's post of 28 October 2026. Replace them with your own.

    Limits
    • Left out by default. Dividends on the preferred already outstanding, new common shares, operating costs and taxes. All of them would lower net assets per share. The field for existing dividends is in the assumptions.
    • Simplified new SATS. It is sold today, a fixed share of its face value buys Bitcoin at today's price, and one year of its dividends is deducted.
    • Preferred at par. Claims are counted at face value, with no market discount.
    • Scenario, not forecast. The page shows what has to be true for a result. It is not financial advice.
